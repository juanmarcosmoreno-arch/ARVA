# SYNTHETIC genome and simulated reads — not biological data

Everything in this folder was generated by `scripts/make_synthetic_genome.py`. The sequences are
random (no genes, no biological meaning) and every FASTA/FASTQ header contains the word `SYNTHETIC`.
Use them to exercise assembly and assembly-quality steps, where the true genome is known
(`reference.fasta`). Do not interpret any result biologically.

Only this README is kept in git; regenerate the data with:

```bash
python3 scripts/make_synthetic_genome.py examples/synthetic-genome
```

The same seed, parameters, numpy and zlib versions give byte-identical files (generated here with
Python 3.13.17, numpy 2.5.4; random generator `numpy.random.default_rng`, PCG64,
with independent streams for the genome, the nanopore reads and the Illumina reads spawned from the seed).

## Genome (`reference.fasta`)

| Replicon | Length (bp) | Topology | Copies | GC (%) | Repeat copies (1-based, inclusive) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SYNTHETIC_chromosome` | 300,000 | circular | 1 | 49.83 | 60,001-65,000, 180,001-185,000 |
| `SYNTHETIC_plasmid` | 15,000 | circular | 2 | 45.44 | - |

- Bases are drawn independently; the probability of G or C at each position follows the mean GC plus
  two sinusoids (amplitudes 6 and 3 percentage points, about one period per 100 kb and per 10 kb,
  whole periods per replicon so that the profile is continuous across the origin), clipped to 20–80 %.
- The chromosome carries two identical copies of one 5,000 bp random sequence (same orientation,
  starting at 20 % and 60 % of its length), so the assembly graph has a repeat to resolve. Nanopore
  reads longer than the repeat plus its flanks can span it; Illumina pairs (inserts around
  350 bp) cannot, so a short-read assembly graph keeps it as one node at about twice the depth.
- Copy number scales the share of reads drawn from the plasmid (reads are sampled in proportion to
  length × copies).

## Nanopore-like reads (`ont.fastq.gz`)

- Reads: 2,248; bases: 16,437,115; median length 6,014 bp, mean
  7,312 bp, N50 8,696 bp, longest 45,866 bp.
- Lengths: log-normal with median 6,000 bp and σ = 0.6 (natural log), at least
  200 bp and at most the replicon length. Start positions are uniform on the circle and reads
  wrap across the origin: 124 reads cross it. Strand: + or − with equal probability.
- Errors: each read gets a target error rate (log-normal around 0.04, σ = 0.3, clipped to
  0.005–0.2). Each reference base gets a Phred quality Q ~ Normal(μ, 4.0) rounded and clipped to
  1–50, with μ chosen so that the mean of 10^(−Q/10) equals the target; the base is then in
  error with probability 10^(−Q/10). An error is a substitution, an insertion (the base is followed by
  a random base with the same Q) or a deletion (the base is dropped and the next base's Q is lowered
  to the deleted base's Q) with probabilities 0.4/0.25/0.35.
- Realised: 265,270 substitutions, 165,789 insertions, 232,234 deletions =
  4.02 % of 16,503,560 reference bases; the mean error probability implied by
  the qualities is 4.09 %.

## Illumina-like paired-end reads (`illumina_R1.fastq.gz`, `illumina_R2.fastq.gz`)

- Pairs: 66,000 of 2 × 150 bp (19,800,000 bases); read names match between the files
  (`/1`, `/2`).
- Fragments: start uniform on the circle (wrapping across the origin: 197 fragments),
  insert size Normal(350, 50) rounded and clipped to the read length … replicon length
  (realised mean 349.8, SD 50.2); R1 reads the fragment's 5′ end, R2 the
  reverse complement of its 3′ end; fragment strand + or − with equal probability. No adapters.
- Errors: substitutions only. Q ~ Normal(μ_position, 5.0) rounded and clipped to
  2–41, where μ gives a mean error probability rising linearly from
  0.25 % at the first base to 0.75 % at the last
  (mean 0.50 %); each base is substituted with probability 10^(−Q/10).
- Realised: 99,009 substitutions = 0.500 % of bases; the mean error
  probability implied by the qualities is 0.501 %.

## Depth

Read bases drawn from each replicon ÷ its length, i.e. the coverage an assembler sees; in brackets the
expectation, depth target × copies (targets: 50.0× nanopore and 60.0× Illumina per copy,
over 330,000 bp counting every copy).

| Replicon | Copies | Nanopore (×) | Illumina (×) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SYNTHETIC_chromosome` | 1 | 50.5 (50) | 60.0 (60) |
| `SYNTHETIC_plasmid` | 2 | 90.3 (100) | 120.2 (120) |
